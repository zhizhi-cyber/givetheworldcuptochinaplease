About This Vote

Why China vs. India?

China and India are the only two nations with 1.4-billion-plus populations. Their football development paths are dramatically different, making this comparison inherently discussion-worthy.

Why is repeat voting allowed?

This site measures the intensity of ongoing support, not a verified count of unique signatures. Every click is an expression of opinion.
